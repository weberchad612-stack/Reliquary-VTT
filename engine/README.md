# engine

The game-system-independent logic behind the board and the tools: maps and grid, walls, sight and fog, light, movement and terrain, tokens, areas, visual effects, sound, the rules machinery, dice and time (calendars, clocks, durations, encounters). The board page itself is in `board/`.

The engine has no game-system or adventure knowledge. Rulesets fill its extension points.

## Modules

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| `walls/presets.js` | The nine wall types and their movement, sight, light and sound values; door, secret-door and veil behaviour; 1/8 cell snapping (1/32 for print-resolution imports); one-way sides. |
| `doors/leaf.js` | Door leaves for drawing: `leafShapes` (closed panel, open panel swung on its hinge, swing arc, centre), `leafEnds`, `pairOf` (the other leaf of a double door), `sideNormal`. |
| `terrain/terrain.js` | The terrain layer: `TerrainMap` (areas that apply now, by square), `entered` (areas a move enters), `terrainEnterCost` (the movement `enterCost` option from a ruleset), `routeTerrain` (extra cost and checks along a route), `areaCells`, `cellsToRects`. |
| `light/light.js` | Light: `lightShape` (where a light reaches through walls; cones; a thin dim fringe, `DIM_FRINGE`, for bright-only lights), `lightPatch`, `LightMap` (the rule level at every point from base, regions and lights, plus for drawing only a brightness `glow` that fades across dim light and at region edges, `REGION_FADE_FT`, scaled by intensity, and the mixed `colour` of coloured light), `LightCache` (recomputes a light only when it moves or doors change; colour and intensity ride along), `parseColour`, `DARK`/`DIM`/`BRIGHT`. `fog/view.js` `partyView` takes a light map and the ruleset's light rules to work out what the party perceives. |
| `light/animate.js` | Light animation, first set (torch, flicker, pulse, ghostly, chroma): `animationAt` (brightness factor and hue turn for a moment, seeded per light), `animationOf`, `noise`, `ANIMATION_TYPES`, `ANIMATION_DEFAULTS`. Drawing only. |
| `light/daylight.js` | Daylight indoors: `daylightLights` turns every window, grate, arrow slit and open door with open sky on one side into half-circle lights facing into the room (`DAYLIGHT`: bright 15 ft then dim 50 ft by day, dim 15 ft at dusk, none at night); `skyTest`. |
| `measure/measure.js` | Distances: `gridDistance` (diagonal patterns such as 5-10-5), `rulerLength` (through waypoints), `spaceDistance` (between creatures, for reach and range). |
| `areas/areas.js` | Area templates: `burstCells`, `coneCells`, `emanationCells`, `lineCells` (diagonals by the system's pattern), `aimArea` (aim from a caster toward a point, as the mouse does: cones in 8 directions from the caster's edge, lines at any angle, bursts on grid corners), `effectBlockers` and `reachedCells` (line of effect: walls that block movement, minus the system's `passes` list; open doors never block), `buildArea`, `tokensInCells`, `cornerDistance`, `describeArea`. |
| `fx/fx.js` | Visual effects, genre-neutral: `PRIMITIVES` (glow, ring, sparks, orb, streak, arc, bolt, text, glyphs, wedge, fill, shake, flash, beam, scanlines, glitch, muzzle, tracer, rocket, explosion, shockwave, missile; each draws itself at a progress 0 to 1), `planEffect` (a library effect and a play to timed jobs: positions, the shared hit / crit / miss results, floating text, repeats, reduced motion), `resolveEffect` (pack, ruleset, engine, then fallbacks by kind and trait), `checkLibrary`, `FxPlayer` (draws jobs each frame), `makeRng` (seeded, so every screen matches), `ENGINE_EFFECTS`, `BASE_PALETTE`. Libraries of named effects live in the rulesets. |
| `sound/sound.js` | Sound for any game: `SoundMixer` (Web Audio: master and channels for music, ambience, effects and the interface; buffered one-shots with gain, pan, a low-pass and a delay; steerable loops; music streamed on media elements and crossfaded), `bagIndex` (shuffle bags: every piece once, never twice in a row), `musicAt` (the piece and offset every screen works out from one shared record), `chooseMood`, `falloff`, `hearing` (the loudest of several listeners, muffled by walls), `screenPan`, `activeAt` (day or night). |
| `rules/dice.js` | Dice for the rules' own rolls (persistent damage, flat checks, recovery checks): formulas like `2d6+3`, and a seeded random source for repeatable rolls. |
| `time/calendar.js`, `time/clock.js`, `time/encounter.js` | Time for any system: calendars (months, weeks, leap days; the system gives its own) and the clock in seconds; durations and when an effect runs out (time passed, then the start or end of the turn it began in, or the end of the round, as Foundry PF2E counts); encounters (initiative order with the system's tiebreak, rounds and turns, delay and resume, defeated creatures skipped, hidden ones, previous-turn and previous-round corrections, next round) producing turn-start, turn-end, round-end and encounter-end events for the system's rules. |
| `rules/check.js` | Checks for any system, as a roll dialog needs them: `checkRows` (a statistic's parts and modifiers, on or off), `adjustCheck` (with rows switched and modifiers added, under the system's stacking), `rollCheck` (one die, or two keeping the higher or lower), `countedParts`, `notesFor` (notes that trigger on a roll, a feat's own text when the note says so). |
| `rules/expr.js`, `rules/rules.js` | System-free rules: a safe expression language for rule values; predicates; `makeStatistic` (a base plus modifiers with bonus types, the stacking a ruleset gives, and a breakdown of what counted and why), `collectRules` (rule elements: modifier, resistance, weakness, immunity, speed, sense, note, and the system's own keys; unknown keys reported), `degreeOf` (degrees of success with natural 20 and 1), `prepareActor` (a creature from its data, its base stat block, its items and conditions, by the ruleset's `prepare`). |
| `sight/visibility.js` | The sight engine: `prepare` (segments for one sense, with door states applied; a thick wall becomes its box faces, or for sight its far face and end caps per viewer: `wallBox`, `farFace`), `visPolygon` (area visible from a point; unlimited range by default, stopping at the map's edge), `canSee` (line of sight between two points), `footprintPoints`, `viewpoints`, `tokenSight` and `tokenCanSee` (sight for tokens of any size, Tiny to Gargantuan), plus polygon helpers. |
| `movement/movement.js` | Movement: `canStep` (one 8-way step of a token's whole space, no corner cutting), `fits` (does a space fit), `path` (cheapest route), `pathVia` (cheapest route through waypoints, the diagonal count carrying on), `reach` (every position within a movement allowance) and `strideReach` (exactly where one, two and three Strides reach: leftover feet lost, diagonal count kept through the turn). Diagonal costs, difficult terrain, occupied squares and squeezing come from the ruleset as options. |
| `fog/explored.js` | Explored areas: `ExploredMask` (a 1/8-cell bitmap) and `mergeExplored`, which adds what the party sees now to what it has seen before and stores the result as outlines that round-trip exactly. `displayPolys` / `displayOutlines` give straightened outlines for painting. |
| `fog/view.js` | `partyView` (what PCs see now and which tokens players may see; with `canLook` and `alwaysShown`, one creature's own view), `fogStateAt` (visible, explored or hidden) and `paintFog` (paints the player or GM fog onto a canvas; remembered areas dark for players). |
| `tokens/tiny.js` | Tiny tokens: `layoutTiny` (centred when alone, even inside a big creature; a stable quadrant each when sharing with other Tiny tokens, up to four, or with a Small or Medium creature), `spaceOf` (space for sight and drawing) and `moveSpaceOf` (space for movement). |
| `tokens/occupancy.js` | Sharing space: `mayShare` and `blockersAt` under the table rule (a footprint at least two squares wider may share; Tiny shares with anyone). |
| `tokens/display.js` | How big a token is drawn in its space: Small creatures (`small: true`) at 0.75 of a Medium token, centred. |

All are dependency-free ES modules that run in the browser and in Node. Tools reach them through `tools/sight.mjs`, `tools/move.mjs`, `tools/fog.mjs` and `tools/area.mjs`.

Design: [docs/design/maps.md](../docs/design/maps.md). The board page that uses these modules is in `board/` (design: [docs/design/board.md](../docs/design/board.md)).
