Try this

  • Drag Kerra or Pip. The green area is their 25 ft move; the line shows the cheapest route and its cost (5-10-5 diagonals).
  • Click the red door on the middle wall to open it, then walk into the doorway. The ogre appears only while someone can see it.
  • Walk back. The far room stays dimmed: explored, but not visible now.
  • Look through the portcullis (dashed, lower left) and the window. Sight passes, movement does not.
  • Switch to GM view and click any token, even the ogre, to shade what it can see.
  • In GM view, click the gold secret panel to mark it found, then click again to open it.
  • In GM view, drag the three rats (south-east room) into one square. A lone rat sits in the centre; sharing rats take a quadrant each.
